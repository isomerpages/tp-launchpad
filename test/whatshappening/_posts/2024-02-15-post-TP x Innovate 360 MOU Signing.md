<p>TP and Innovate 360 signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) on 11 November
2021 to assist startups in the food-tech space!</p>
<p>&nbsp;This partnership enables both parties to collaborate on programmes,
workshop and events. Startups can also tap on Innovate 360 and Temasek
Polytechnic co-working spaces and incubation-related facilities such as
food labs and test kitchens, augmented by mentorship.</p>
<p>&nbsp;To kick start the partnership, a panel discussion was organized
with the panellist:</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Mr John Cheng, Founder &amp;
Director of @Innovate360</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Dr. Goh Lay Beng, Directory
of School of Applied Science of @Temasekpoly</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Mr Keith Tan, Co-founder &amp;
Chief product Office of @zeemart_official</p>
<p>-&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Ms Seah Jie Ling, Senior Manager
of SME’s go digital &nbsp;@imdasg</p>
<p>&nbsp;The panellist shared about how companies can support food sustainability
by tapping on digital transformation and also how education plays a critical
role in preparing students for such trends.&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;Thank you for those who have joined and hope you had a fruitful
time with us. We would also like to thank our panellist for their sharing!</p>
